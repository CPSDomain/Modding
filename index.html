Every block and item in the mod: what it does, how to make it, and how it fits together. Power runs on Forge Energy, so it works with Mekanism, Thermal, AE2 and the rest of your pack.

Where to start

The mod is built as a ladder. Each rung pays for the next.

  1. Coal

    Burn coal for your first 40 FE/t. Run Copper Cable to an Electric Furnace and a Battery Box.

    Coal Generator, Copper Cable, Electric Furnace, Battery Box

  2. Steam

    Make steel, then a Boiler with a Steam Turbine on top. A Sink next to the boiler gives it endless water; lava underneath heats it for free.

    Steel Ingot, Boiler, Steam Turbine, Sink

  3. Processing

    Double your ores with the Pulverizer, then triple them with the Ore Purifier. The Alloy Smelter makes Superconductor from copper and lithium.

    Pulverizer, Ore Purifier, Alloy Smelter

  4. Fission

    Mine uranium, make Fuel Rods and build a Fission Reactor with a Sink for cooling water. Pipe its steam to turbines for extra power.

    Uranium Ore, Uranium Fuel Rod, Fission Reactor

  5. Fusion

    Mine lithium below Y=0. Fill fuel cells in the Electrolyzer and light a compact Fusion Reactor with 1,000,000 FE.

    Lithium Ore, Electrolyzer, Fusion Reactor

  6. Scale up

    Build the multiblock Fusion Reactor and an Energy Bank, then automate everything with Item Pipes, a Quarry and a Chunk Loader.

    Fusion Controller, Bank Controller, Basic Item Pipe, Powered Quarry

Wind Turbine

A big three-blade rotor that spins while it makes power. The higher you build it, the more it makes: nothing at sea level (Y 64), rising to full power at Y 256. Rain gives x1.5 and thunderstorms x2. Blocks within 3 blocks of it slow it down, so put it on a hill or tower with open space. Its GUI shows height, weather and how open the space is.

up to 256 FE/t (512 in storms)

Boiler

Boils water into steam for Steam Turbines. Heat comes from any furnace fuel or lava in its tank (full speed), or free from lava, magma, fire or a lit campfire directly underneath (half speed). Water comes from a Sink, Pump, pipes, tanks or buckets. Steam goes straight into a turbine on top, or out through Gas Pipes on any side to turbines anywhere.

Fission Reactor

The compact, single-block fission generator - and the core ingredient of the Fission Controller for the multiblock reactor. Burns up to 4 Uranium Fuel Rods (5 minutes each; they come back as Depleted Fuel Rods). Output rises with heat and is full at 50%. Above that it cools itself with water from a Sink, Pump or pipes - and the water flashes to steam, which it pushes into Gas Pipes or Steam Turbines for bonus power. Without water it overheats and SCRAMs (shuts itself down until cool). Meltdown explosions are off unless you turn them on in the config.

How to make it

Item Filter

Right-click the air to set up to 9 items, choose Allow or Block, and choose how to match: Item (exactly those items), Tag (anything sharing a tag - one raw ore matches all raw ores, one log matches all logs) or Mod (anything from the same mod). Right-click an Item Pipe connection with it to install it; shift + right-click the connection with an empty hand to take it off. A delivering connection then only accepts matching items; a pulling connection only pulls them.

How to make it

Storage Terminal

Shows everything stored in the network in one searchable grid. Click to take a stack, right-click to take half, shift-click to send it straight to your inventory. Click the grid while holding an item to store it (right-click stores just one), or shift-click items in your inventory to store them. Fluids and gases show too (amounts in buckets): hold an empty bucket or tank and click a fluid to fill it, or click the grid with a full one to pour it in. Type to search by name, or @modname for one mod. The All / Items / Fluids button picks what to show; 123 / A-Z switches the sort.

How to make it

Storage Terminal Panel

A flat Storage Terminal for walls, floors and ceilings, like AE2's. Place it on a Storage Cable or any storage block; the screen faces away from the surface you click. Craft it from a Storage Terminal block, and back again.

How to make it

Crafting Terminal

A Storage Terminal with a 3x3 crafting grid underneath. Craft straight from storage: when a craft uses up a grid slot it refills from storage, so shift-clicking the result crafts as many as your stored materials allow. The x button (or closing the terminal) puts the grid back into storage. With JEI, open any crafting recipe and press + to fill the grid from storage; shift + fills a stack of each. Missing ingredients show in red.

How to make it

Crafting Terminal Panel

A flat Crafting Terminal. Works exactly like the block version. Craft it from a Crafting Terminal block (and back), or from a Storage Terminal Panel plus a crafting table.

How to make it

Import Bus

Moves things FROM the block it faces INTO storage. Place it against a chest, furnace, machine or tank (it faces the block you click on) and connect it to the network with Storage Cable. It takes items, fluids and gases. Right-click to set a filter; with an empty filter it takes everything. To filter a fluid, click a filter slot with a bucket or tank of it. The screen shows whether it's connected to a powered network.

How to make it

Export Bus

Moves things FROM storage INTO the block it faces - it never takes anything out of that block. Right-click and set what to send: items, or a bucket/tank for a fluid or gas. With an empty filter it sends nothing. Good for keeping a Pulverizer fed with ore, a Coal Generator topped up with coal, or a Steam Turbine supplied with stored steam.

How to make it

Storage Interface

A doorway into storage from anywhere on the Storage Cable. Anything next to it - Item Pipes, Fluid and Gas Pipes, machines, reactors, hoppers - can push items, fluids and gases into storage or pull them out, as if it were one huge chest and tank. Put one next to a Fission Reactor to store its steam, and an Export Bus on a Steam Turbine elsewhere to use it.

How to make it

Wireless Terminal

Your storage in your hand. Sneak + right-click a Storage Controller to link it, then right-click anywhere within 64 blocks (in the same dimension) to open a full Storage Terminal - items, fluids, search and all. It runs on FE, using a little while it's open; charge it in a Charger, Wireless Charger or Portable Battery. Set the Open Wireless Terminal key in Controls > Key Binds > Tiered Power and you can open it straight from your inventory.

How to make it

Energy Meter

Goes in a cable line. Power goes in the LEFT side and out the RIGHT side as you look at its screen (the top shows an arrow). The screen shows FE per tick and a running total; right-click for the peak as well, sneak + right-click to reset. A redstone signal switches the flow off, so it doubles as a power switch for a whole branch of your base. A comparator next to it gets a stronger signal the more power flows.

Power Monitor

Gases and steam

Hydrogen, oxygen and steam travel in Gas Pipes and can be kept in Fluid Tanks. Split water into hydrogen and oxygen, burn it later for power, or put spare steam to work.

Gas Electrolyzer

Splits water into hydrogen and oxygen using power. Pulls water from a Sink, Electric Pump or pipes and pushes both gases out into Gas Pipes, Fluid Tanks or a Gas Burner Generator. Put a Hydrogen Jetpack in its slot to refuel it. Speed and Efficiency Upgrades work here.

Power

How to make it

Gas Burner Generator

Burns hydrogen for power; add oxygen as well for a 30% boost. You get back less than the Gas Electrolyzer used, so treat hydrogen as a way to store and move energy - for example run the electrolyzer on solar by day and burn the hydrogen at night, or pipe it to the far side of your base. Idles when its buffer is full, so no gas is wasted.
